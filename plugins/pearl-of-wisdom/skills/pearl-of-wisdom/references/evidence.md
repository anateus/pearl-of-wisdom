# Evidence discipline

Record an observation with its source, collection time, population or sample frame, units, and transformation. Record a hypothesis or assumption separately. Include negative and missing observations when they affect the question. For a probabilistic analysis, preserve the inputs and model version so another turn can recompute it.

Do not promote any of these into evidence without independent support:

- Hindsight pages, agent memories, or prior conversation context.
- A prior chosen for convenience.
- A Monte Carlo sample generated from assumptions.
- Correlation, a prediction, or a model's posterior when the claim is causal.

When evidence conflicts, retain both entries and explain the resolution or unresolved uncertainty. A durable record should make it possible to tell what was measured, what was inferred, and what remains unknown.
