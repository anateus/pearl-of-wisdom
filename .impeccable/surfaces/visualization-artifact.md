# Pearl visualization artifact

Mode: Read.

## THESIS

Treat each artifact as an audit sheet for one computation. The chart explains shape or interval; the surrounding document preserves the numbers, provenance, and limits needed to check it.

## OWN-WORLD

This is an extension of Pearl's technical output, not a replacement identity or an own-world page. Portability and exact reading take priority over custom brand assets. The visual language is warm paper, dark ink, thin rules, one cobalt data color, an editorial serif for headings, and a restrained sans serif for working text.

## STORY

The reader first identifies the quantity and unit, then reads the chart, then checks retained-draw coverage and authoritative full-run statistics. Provenance and calibration limits close the sheet. Forest artifacts use the same order while allowing individual scenarios to be hidden without changing their summaries.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The quantity and unit lead directly into a chart that uses most of the available width. Full-run counts and summaries sit directly below it. At narrow widths, the chart contracts to the viewport and each summary row becomes a labeled vertical record, so no value depends on horizontal clipping.

## Constraints

Static HTML preserves title, full-run summary, source hash, version details, and caveats when JavaScript is unavailable. Capped exports visibly separate retained draw shape from full-run statistics. The chart may not infer units, rank scenarios, pool unequal populations, calculate forest intervals from retained draws, or suppress subset and calibration caveats. Inline, shared, and CDN resource modes keep the same document structure and statistical meaning.
